应用说明

Cycle Life Advisor 是一款基于 Apple 健康数据与 AI 的生活向周期助手应用，帮助你了解周期阶段，并通过 AI 对话获得生活方式参考。

重要：本应用仅供参考，不构成医疗建议、诊断或治疗。如有健康问题，请咨询合格医疗人员。

常见问题
  • 看不到周期数据？ 请在「健康」App 中记录经期相关数据，并在系统设置中授予本应用读取权限。
  • AI 助手无法回复？ 请检查网络连接；若问题持续，请通过上方邮箱联系我们并说明大致时间与界面提示。
  • 数据存在哪里？ 详见隐私政策：健康数据以设备本地为主，具体以应用实际行为为准。

About the App

Cycle Life Advisor is a lifestyle-oriented cycle wellness app that uses Apple Health data and AI chat to provide reference guidance based on your cycle phase.

Important: The App is for informational purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified professional for health concerns.

FAQ
  • No cycle data? Log cycle-related data in the Health app and grant this App read access in Settings.
  • AI Assistant not replying? Check your network connection. If it persists, email us with the approximate time and any on-screen message.
  • Where is my data stored? See our Privacy Policy — health data is primarily on-device; details follow the App’s actual behavior.
